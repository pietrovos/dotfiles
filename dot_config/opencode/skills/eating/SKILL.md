# Eating

Keep what Pietro has said about food and supplements in `references/foods.md` alongside this skill. The file persists across sessions; read it before answering anything food or supplement related, and update it when he gives new information.

## Read and use the record

1. Read `references/foods.md` first.
2. When he asks whether something is safe for him, compare its ingredients with the record: name the ingredients he already tolerates, the ones that have bothered him, and the ones the record says nothing about. Say plainly when there is no data rather than guessing.
3. A tolerated product is evidence for its ingredients, not proof: dose and combination matter. Say so when an answer leans on it.
4. Give general nutrition or supplement information when asked, but do not diagnose. If he describes severe, persistent or worsening symptoms (blood, vomiting, weight loss, pain that wakes him), tell him to see a doctor.

## Save and correct

- Save what he states about food and supplements: products and ingredients that do or don't hurt his stomach, other reactions, preferences, dislikes, diets, supplements he takes and their doses.
- When he shares a label photo, transcribe the product name and the full ingredient list. Note anything not readable instead of guessing.
- Record the date and his own words for each reaction. Keep "fine", "bothered me" and "unsure" separate; one bad day after a meal is not the same as a confirmed trigger, so record what he said, not a conclusion.
- When he corrects or reverses something, update the entry and note the change.
- Keep the file short and organized by section; don't duplicate entries. Keep an ingredient index so tolerance can be checked by ingredient.
- After changes, sync the original skill directory with chezmoi following chezmoi-tracking. The Claude Code link points to the same directory, so edits need only be made once. Do not commit or push unless requested.
- Briefly confirm what was saved.
